HOW TO PUT FLASH SHEETS ON THE WALL
===================================

Drop the picture into the artist's  Designs  folder:

    KIOSK MEDIA / <Artist name> / Designs /

It shows on the wall within about a minute, under that artist and in View
All. Everything dropped in counts as a flash sheet. (A picture dropped loose
straight into the artist's own folder counts too.)

  - Your file is never changed. The wall uses a resized copy kept in the
    _kiosk folder inside Designs - leave _kiosk alone.
  - Very small pictures (under 1080 pixels on the long side) are skipped.
  - To take a sheet off the wall, delete the file or move it out.

Headshots go in  <Artist name> / Headshots  (set from the dashboard).
