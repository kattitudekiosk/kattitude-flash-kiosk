HOW TO PUT FLASH ON THE WALL
============================

Drop the picture into the artist's  Flash  folder:

    KIOSK MEDIA / <Artist name> / Flash /

That's it. It shows on the wall within about a minute, under that artist
and in View All.

  - Exactly 2048 x 2048 pixels  ->  a single design
  - Anything else               ->  a flash sheet
  - Very small pictures (under 1080 pixels on the long side) are skipped.

Your file is never changed. The wall uses a resized copy that lives in the
_kiosk folder next to it - leave _kiosk alone.

To take something off the wall, delete (or move out) the file.

Headshots go in  <Artist name> / Headshots  (set from the dashboard).

The old "Designs" and "Sheets" folders are gone. Anything put in a folder
with one of those names is moved into Flash automatically.
